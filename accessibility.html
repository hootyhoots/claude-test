Accessibility

StreamVibe is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people of all abilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone.

Our Commitment

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. We regularly audit our platform and work to address any gaps.

Features

  • Closed captions and subtitles available on all content
  • Audio descriptions for select titles
  • Keyboard navigation support throughout the platform
  • Screen reader compatibility
  • Customizable subtitle appearance (size, color, background)
  • High contrast mode support

Feedback

If you encounter any accessibility barriers, please contact us. We welcome your feedback and will work to address your concerns.
