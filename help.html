Help Center

Find answers to common questions or get in touch with our support team.

How do I reset my password?

Go to the Sign In page and click "Forgot password?" to receive a reset link via email.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from your account settings. Your access will continue until the end of your billing period.

What devices are supported?

StreamVibe works on smart TVs, smartphones, tablets, laptops, gaming consoles, and streaming devices.

Can I download content for offline viewing?

Yes! Standard and Premium plans include download capability for mobile devices.

How do I change my plan?

Visit your account settings to upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! All new members get a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime during the trial and you won't be charged.
